# Living World Vision — Florianópolis as a co-created playable territory

> Public concept record for Ilha Floripa Digital.

## Core idea

Ilha Floripa Digital is not intended to be only a 3D reconstruction of Florianópolis.

The long-term direction is a **living, playable and cinematic digital territory of Santa Catarina Island that can learn from the real city and evolve with the participation of its inhabitants, visitors and players**.

The real city is not only the setting. It becomes one of the sources that continuously helps build the digital world.

## The loop

```text
REAL FLORIANÓPOLIS
        ↓
photos · video · audio · stories · scans · events · local knowledge
        ↓
multimodal AI
        ↓
interpret · geolocate · classify · compare · moderate · relate
        ↓
staging / review layer
        ↓
author or community validation
        ↓
DIGITAL WORLD
        ↓
assets · memories · stories · events · environment updates · gameplay
        ↓
cinematic and interactive experiences
        ↓
new real-world encounters and contributions
        ↺
```

## Player as co-creator

A player should eventually be able to contribute to the development of the world without needing to become a traditional game developer.

Examples:

- photographing a street can provide reference for materials, façades and urban detail;
- recording a trail can help improve terrain, vegetation and path information;
- filming a place can provide temporal, lighting, movement and spatial references;
- recording oral history can become a geolocated memory layer;
- documenting an event can create a time-based story or world event;
- validating a reconstruction can improve the accuracy and cultural relevance of the digital place.

The principle is that **walking, observing, documenting and telling stories can become forms of worldbuilding**.

## Cinematic interaction with real life

The world should not expose real places and people only through pins, ads or database cards.

Information can be transformed into cinematic interactions: spatial audio, camera transitions, guided viewpoints, environmental storytelling, reconstructed moments, before/after layers, local voices and contextual discovery.

A restaurant, artist, architect, photographer, guide, musician, researcher, community project or cultural space can become discoverable through the world itself.

## Living portfolio

Residents and local agents may eventually use their presence in the world as a **living portfolio**.

Possible entities include:

- people and creators;
- places and businesses;
- cultural and social projects;
- architecture and design;
- music and audiovisual work;
- guides and local knowledge;
- research and environmental initiatives.

The objective is not conventional advertising placed on top of the map. The aim is **contextual presence**: work, stories and services discovered through the place, its history and the player's experience.

## World memory

Every place may accumulate layers of memory:

- geography;
- architecture;
- ecology;
- historical imagery;
- oral histories;
- artworks;
- events;
- community contributions;
- different moments in time.

This creates the possibility of a city that can eventually be explored not only in space, but also through **time and human memory**.

## System architecture

The concept is organized around seven complementary systems:

1. **Living World** — navigable and playable 3D territory.
2. **World Capture** — photos, video, audio, scans and field observations.
3. **World Intelligence** — multimodal AI for interpretation, comparison and organization.
4. **World Memory** — temporal, cultural and geolocated memory.
5. **World Cinema** — cinematic presentation and interaction systems.
6. **Living Portfolio** — contextual presence for people, places and projects.
7. **World Governance** — consent, authorship, permissions, provenance, moderation and removal.

## Safety and governance principle

No public contribution should flow directly from upload to the playable world.

```text
CAPTURE → AI ANALYSIS → STAGING → REVIEW / RIGHTS CHECK → WORLD
```

The pipeline must progressively account for privacy, minors, faces, private property, sensitive locations, copyright, misinformation, commercial spam, cultural context and withdrawal requests.

## Development ambition

The project explores a next-generation living-world experience combining:

- real geography;
- high-quality 3D environments;
- procedural systems;
- multimodal AI;
- persistent world memory;
- community co-creation;
- cinematic interaction;
- local cultural and economic discovery.

This is a research and development direction, not a claim that all of these systems already exist.

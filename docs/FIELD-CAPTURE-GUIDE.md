# Field Capture Guide

> Minimum practical requirements for contributing real-world references to Ilha Floripa Digital.

The goal is to make participation possible with ordinary equipment while preserving enough information for useful AI-assisted reconstruction, comparison and worldbuilding.

## Level 0 — Minimum viable contribution

A modern smartphone is enough for many useful contributions.

### Minimum capabilities

- camera capable of at least 1080p video;
- still photos at normal phone resolution;
- location services available;
- enough storage to preserve original files;
- automatic image filters or beauty effects disabled when possible.

### Capture requirements

1. Keep the original files and metadata whenever possible.
2. Record the location accurately enough to identify the place.
3. State what is being documented and why.
4. Capture context before details.
5. Avoid only taking extreme zoom or cropped shots.
6. Record multiple angles when documenting geometry.
7. Do not trespass or enter restricted/private areas without permission.
8. Avoid deliberately capturing identifiable people who have not agreed to participate.

## Level 1 — Recommended field kit

For regular contributors:

- smartphone with 4K video and optical stabilization;
- power bank;
- small tripod or handheld stabilizer;
- lens-cleaning cloth;
- optional external microphone;
- optional measurement tape or compact laser distance meter;
- notes app or field form for context.

This level should be enough for most community documentation, façade references, trails, viewpoints, environmental observations and oral-memory contributions.

## Level 2 — Advanced capture

Useful when higher geometric or audiovisual fidelity is required:

- mirrorless / DSLR camera;
- 360° camera;
- calibrated or well-documented lenses;
- dedicated audio recorder;
- gimbal / tripod;
- color reference card;
- scale markers;
- LiDAR-capable phone/tablet or dedicated scanner;
- photogrammetry capture workflow.

## Level 3 — Professional / research capture

For specific mapping, surveying or production tasks:

- survey-grade or RTK GNSS;
- high-resolution LiDAR;
- calibrated camera systems;
- drone / aerial capture where legally authorized;
- spatial audio rigs;
- controlled photogrammetry systems;
- environmental or oceanographic sensors when relevant.

Professional equipment is **not** a prerequisite for general participation.

## Suggested capture patterns

### Building / façade

- one wide contextual image;
- front elevation where accessible;
- oblique views from both sides;
- close material/detail shots;
- short slow video sweep;
- approximate location and orientation.

### Street or path

- wide shot showing direction;
- forward-facing walking video;
- reverse direction reference;
- intersections / entrances;
- notable surface, vegetation and furniture details.

### Landscape / viewpoint

- exact standing point if safe;
- compass heading or approximate direction;
- wide image;
- several overlapping images;
- optional panorama;
- time/date and weather context.

### Perspective Anchor contribution

When documenting the viewpoint behind a painting, photograph or film frame, record if possible:

- the reference work itself;
- creator / source / rights information;
- approximate real viewpoint;
- phone compass heading;
- camera height;
- wide scene image;
- best-effort matching image;
- date or historical period of the original work.

### Oral memory

- identify the place being discussed;
- obtain clear permission to record and submit;
- record in a reasonably quiet environment;
- preserve the original audio;
- optionally capture a short contextual image of the place.

## File handling

Prefer original files over social-media downloads because platforms often remove metadata and recompress images.

Recommended information accompanying a contribution:

```text
place:
date/time:
approximate location:
direction / viewpoint:
what is documented:
why it matters:
author / contributor:
rights / permission status:
notes:
```

## Privacy, rights and safety

A useful contribution must also be a responsible one.

Do not submit material that requires unauthorized access, exposes sensitive personal information, reveals protected/sensitive locations, or reproduces copyrighted material without an appropriate basis for use.

Drone operations and other regulated capture methods must follow applicable Brazilian rules and local restrictions. Contributors are responsible for lawful field operation.

## Design principle

**Participation should start with the device already in someone's pocket.** More advanced equipment increases precision, but it should not be the gatekeeper for community worldbuilding.

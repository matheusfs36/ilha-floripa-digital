# Mechanics Catalog

> Public registry of gameplay and interaction concepts for Ilha Floripa Digital.

This file exists so important mechanics do not disappear inside conversations or prototypes. Items are concepts and research targets unless explicitly marked implemented.

## M001 — Perspective Anchor / Viewpoint Match

**Status:** priority concept

Connect an artwork, photograph, film frame, architectural drawing or historical image to the specific real-world viewpoint from which it was created.

### Example

A painting of Praia do Moçambique can be placed at the approximate point and orientation where the painter observed the landscape. The player can stand there, align the work with the digital landscape and understand: **this image was born from this view**.

### Useful metadata

- latitude / longitude;
- approximate altitude or camera height;
- heading / azimuth;
- pitch;
- field of view or focal-length estimate;
- capture or creation date / period;
- author and provenance;
- uncertainty / confidence.

### Interaction possibilities

- ghosted overlay between artwork and landscape;
- split-screen or slider alignment;
- camera snapping to the historical/original composition;
- environmental transition between present and past;
- spatial audio or artist narration;
- reward for finding the correct viewpoint;
- AR companion mode in the real location.

### Applications

Art, photography, cinema, architecture, historical memory, education, tourism and before/after reconstruction.

## M002 — Reality Feed / Player-as-Worldbuilder

Players and residents can submit photos, videos, audio, scans and observations from real Florianópolis. Multimodal AI analyzes the material and proposes updates or additions to the digital world.

Contribution does not publish directly. It moves through staging, rights checks and validation.

Possible outputs include:

- façade/material references;
- vegetation observations;
- path and trail changes;
- urban furniture;
- spatial references;
- environmental events;
- stories and memories;
- reconstruction tasks.

---

## M003 — Living Voice / Geolocated Oral Memory

People can record stories, memories and knowledge connected to places. AI can transcribe, summarize, tag, moderate and associate the contribution with geography while preserving the human voice when permission allows.

The player discovers voices through exploration rather than through a conventional feed.

---

## M004 — Time Layers

Places can accumulate multiple temporal states.

A player may eventually explore or compare:

- historical imagery;
- previous buildings;
- older coastlines or landscapes;
- remembered events;
- present-day state;
- speculative or planned futures.

Time becomes another navigation axis of the world.

---

## M005 — Living Portfolio

A person, place, business or project can have a contextual presence inside the world.

Instead of banner advertising, discovery happens through location, story, contribution and experience.

Examples:

- an architect connected to a building they designed;
- a musician connected to a place where a recording happened;
- a photographer connected to viewpoints and images;
- a local maker connected to objects and workshops;
- a social or environmental project connected to its territory.

---

## M006 — Scale Shift

Characters or playable states may operate at different physical scales: human, miniature, giant or experimental.

Scale changes movement, camera, collision, physics, traversal and interaction, allowing the same geography to support radically different experiences.

---

## M007 — Real Event Echo

Selected real-world events can leave temporary or permanent traces in the digital world after verification and rights review.

Examples include cultural events, environmental phenomena, community activities, exhibitions or local celebrations.

---

## M008 — Contribution Provenance

World elements should be able to preserve a traceable history of how they were created or improved.

Possible provenance chain:

```text
source capture → contributor → AI interpretation → reviewer → generated/edited asset → world version
```

This can support credit, correction, trust, research reproducibility and future attribution systems.

---

## M009 — Discover by Place, not Feed

The primary interface for local discovery should be the territory itself.

Instead of an infinite social feed, the player encounters stories, people, work and opportunities through movement, proximity, curiosity and narrative context.

---

## M010 — Real-to-Digital-to-Real Loop

The game can generate reasons to experience Florianópolis physically, and those physical experiences can in turn improve the digital world.

```text
WORLD → curiosity / mission / encounter → REAL CITY → capture / story / experience → WORLD
```

This loop is a core design principle rather than a single feature.

## Adding a mechanic

New mechanics should record:

1. a short name and ID;
2. the player experience;
3. the real-world connection, if any;
4. required data or sensors;
5. privacy / rights considerations;
6. smallest useful prototype;
7. implementation status.

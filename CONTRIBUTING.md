# Contributing

Thanks for being curious about Ilha Floripa Digital.

The project is in an early research phase. Contributions are opening progressively.

## Good contribution shape

Prefer a small, isolated problem with:

1. a clear goal;
2. reproducible inputs;
3. an observable result;
4. acceptance criteria;
5. no dependency on private credentials or restricted assets.

## Areas we expect to open

- WebGPU / GPU compute
- GIS / terrain / bathymetry
- Unreal Engine
- procedural generation
- vegetation
- ocean and coastal simulation
- computer vision / photogrammetry
- optimization / LOD / streaming
- gameplay
- documentation and research

## Before starting

Check the Issues list first.

For larger ideas, open an Issue describing:

- the problem;
- why it matters;
- the proposed approach;
- dependencies;
- a small proof-of-concept target.

## Public/private boundary

Do not submit:

- secrets, tokens or credentials;
- licensed material without redistribution rights;
- machine-specific private configuration;
- large generated caches or Unreal build output;
- private datasets;
- material copied from the internal TDZ laboratory without explicit review.

## License

A project-wide open-source license has not been selected yet.
